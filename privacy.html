Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 17, 2026

Cloud Motion Lab LLC ("we," "our," or "us") built LED Banner: Scrolling Text ("the App") as a commercial application.

Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The App does not require an account, login, or registration. All data, including saved messages, color preferences, font selections, and purchase status, is stored locally on your device.

Camera and Microphone

The App does not access your camera, microphone, photos, contacts, or location.

Advertising

The free version of the App displays ads through Unity Ads. We use Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework to request permission before tracking. If you decline tracking, ads will be non-personalized. No personal data is shared with ad networks without your explicit consent.

In-App Purchases

The App offers a one-time Pro purchase processed through Apple's App Store. All purchase management, including refunds and cancellations, is handled through your Apple ID account settings. We do not have access to your payment information.

Data Storage

All app data, including saved messages, display preferences, and purchase status, is stored locally on your device using SwiftData. This data is not transmitted to any server.

Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

Unity Ads (advertising), Apple StoreKit (in-app purchases). Each service has its own privacy policy governing the data it collects.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at dev@slyapp.co
